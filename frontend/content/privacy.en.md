## 1. Introduction

Salair is committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 2. Data Collected

We collect:
- Identification data: name, email, encrypted password
- Professional data: company name, SIRET number
- Payment data (via Stripe)
- Usage data for analytics and service improvement

## 3. Purpose

Your data is used to:
- Create and manage your account
- Provide and improve our services
- Handle billing and support
- Fulfill legal obligations

## 4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on:
- Your consent (cookies, contact forms)
- Contract execution (subscriptions)
- Legal obligations (accounting, invoicing)

## 5. Data Recipients

We may share data with trusted third-party processors:
- **Stripe** (payments)
- **MinIO** (file storage)
- Technical providers (hosting, emailing)

No data is transferred outside the EU without appropriate safeguards.

## 6. Retention Period

Data is kept as long as your account is active, then archived for 5 years as required by French law.

## 7. Security

Salair uses encryption, access control, and regular security audits to protect your data.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access, modify or delete your data
- Object to or restrict processing
- Request data portability
- Withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise your rights, contact: privacy@salair.fr

## 9. Cookies

Cookies may be used to improve user experience and gather analytics. You can manage your preferences via the cookie banner.
