## 1. Cookie use

Salair only uses **strictly necessary cookies** to ensure the website functions properly. We do not use any advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 2. Types of cookies used

- **Technical cookies**: Essential for site operation (e.g., session management, security, language).
- **Functional cookies**: Used to store user preferences (e.g., light/dark theme).

## 3. Data collected

These cookies do not contain or collect personal data and cannot be used to identify users.

## 4. No consent required

In compliance with GDPR, these cookies are exempt from consent as they are essential for the operation of the site.
